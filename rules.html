Contest rules

Flappy Bikes · Yalla Bikes Tel Aviv

Who can enter

Ages 16 and over. Anyone who can collect the prize in Tel Aviv can enter.

Contest dates

Dates coming soon

Prize

One free day rental of 2 bikes from Yalla Bikes Tel Aviv, to be used within 60 days. The prize is not exchangeable for cash.

How the winner is chosen

The highest All Time score during the contest wins. Ties go to whoever reached that score first.

Fair play

Yalla Bikes may verify the winning score, including a short supervised play session at the shop, and disqualify suspicious or cheating entries.

Privacy

Contact information is used only to reach the winner and is deleted after the contest.

How we contact the winner

We will contact the winner by Instagram DM or phone within 3 days of the contest ending.
